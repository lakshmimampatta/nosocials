# nosocials
It's for controlling the social media usage. is an application that helps users reduce their social media usage and overall screen time. Users can choose how much they want to reduce their screen use and receive support for following that goal. The app also allows users to compare their progress with others and check the sources behind the information provided in the app.
The intended users are people who feel they spend too much time on social media or their phones and want support in reducing their screen use.

I will include 4 persuassive features Reduction, Suggestion, Verifiability and Social Comparison + Competition. There should be a Home, Progress, Leaderboard, and Sources screen. The workflow is: Home -> Progress -> Leaderboard -> Sources
